# Ditto（跟读）隐私政策 / Privacy Policy

更新日期：2026-11-23

## 中文

Ditto（跟读）是一个英语跟读练习 App。我们**不收集、不上传、不出售**任何个人信息，没有账号系统，没有广告，没有第三方统计或追踪。

- **学习记录、收藏、头像和昵称**：保存在你的手机上。如果你开着 iCloud，会同步到**你自己的** iCloud 私有数据库（Apple CloudKit），只有你的 Apple 账户能读取，开发者看不到。
- **麦克风录音**：只在跟读、复述时用来和原声对比打分，在手机上处理，不上传、不保存到别处。
- **语音识别**：使用 Apple 的本机识别，不上传。
- **相册和相机**：只在你主动选视频或拍头像时使用，内容留在手机上。
- **免费课程**：从 GitHub 下载公开的课程文件（美国之音 VOA Learning English，公有领域），下载请求不带任何个人信息。
- **Mac 导入工具（可选）**：运行在你自己的 Mac 上，处理结果放进你自己的 iCloud 云盘，不经过开发者的服务器。

删除 App 即删除手机上的全部数据；iCloud 里的副本可以在「设置 → Apple 账户 → iCloud → 管理存储空间」里删除。

有问题请在 https://github.com/limkown-stack/ditto-library/issues 留言。

## English

Ditto is an English shadowing practice app. It does **not collect, upload or sell** any personal data. There are no accounts, no ads, and no third‑party analytics or tracking.

- **Progress, saved words, avatar and name** stay on your device and, if iCloud is on, sync to **your own** private iCloud database (Apple CloudKit). The developer cannot access it.
- **Microphone** recordings are used only on‑device to score your shadowing, and are not uploaded.
- **Speech recognition** uses Apple's on‑device recognition.
- **Photos and camera** are used only when you pick a video or take an avatar photo; content stays on your device.
- **Free courses** are public files (VOA Learning English, public domain) downloaded from GitHub; requests carry no personal data.
- **The optional Mac companion tool** runs on your own Mac and writes to your own iCloud Drive; nothing passes through developer servers.

Deleting the app removes all on‑device data. iCloud copies can be removed in Settings → Apple Account → iCloud → Manage Storage.

Questions: https://github.com/limkown-stack/ditto-library/issues
